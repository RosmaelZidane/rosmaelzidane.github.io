01 / Research

Understanding code.
Improving security.

My work combines learned code representations, security knowledge, and rigorous evaluations. The goal is not just higher accuracy, but models we can explain, transfer, and use.

01.Knowledge-informed AI

Teach models what vulnerabilities mean.

Inject domain-specific knowledge into graph neural networks so code-security models can reason beyond surface patterns.

Read the IST paper ↗
02.Code relationships

Look beyond individual functions.

Model function-level dependencies and structural context for vulnerability detection, then examine when relational signals help.

Read the EAAI paper ↗
03.Reliability & evaluation

Measure what actually matters.

Study imbalance, interpretability, and practical use of AI-generated warnings, including the conditions under which model predictions fail.

Read the SN CS paper ↗
